# B103 — Rubber-band selection rectangle grows beyond the canvas ✅ fixed
*Archived roadmap item — Bugs. Open items: [../ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) · Index: [README.md](README.md)*

**Reported (08/2026).** Dragging a rubber-band selection could draw the selection rectangle PAST the view/canvas bounds — the rect grew beyond the canvas instead of being clipped at its edges.

**Fixed as part of A38**, as the roadmap predicted it would be. Under DragSelect the rect was positioned by the library against the view as its `area`, with nothing clipping it once the pointer left; in the hand-rolled band the geometry is computed in one place, so clamping is a single expression in `currentBox()`:

```js
left:  Math.max(box.left,  area.left),   right:  Math.min(box.right,  area.right),
top:   Math.max(box.top,   area.top),    bottom: Math.min(box.bottom, area.bottom),
```

Because `currentBox()` feeds **both** the drawn overlay and the hit test, "what you see" and "what you select" stay identical by construction — clamping only the visual rect would have been the subtler bug. `_drawOverlay` also floors the size at 0, since clamping can invert the box when a gesture ends up wholly outside the canvas.

**Verified** in `test-browser/feezal-editor-rubberband.test.js`: dragging far past the bottom-right stops the rect at the view's right/bottom edges, dragging back past the origin clamps top/left, and a band dragged from beyond one corner to beyond the opposite one still selects every tile it covers — the clamp must not cost the selection.

**Relates:** **A38** ✅ (drop DragSelect — this dissolved into it), B2/B35/B48 ✅ (the DragSelect lifecycle bug family).
