# E183 — glass-media element ✅ shipped
*Archived roadmap item — Elements. Open items: [../ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) · Index: [README.md](README.md)*

**Requested (08/2026).** A **glass** member of the media family — today
only `circle-media` and `metro-media` exist, so a frosted dashboard has
no matching media card.

- Same attribute contract as the existing media elements (transport,
  title/artist/album/artwork, volume, shuffle/repeat — plus the mute,
  provider and album/provider-dedupe additions from E182), so the E115
  family switch pairs circle ↔ metro ↔ glass cleanly and discovery
  stamps all three identically.
- Glass chrome: frost card, album art filling the card with the text
  block over a scrim (the Home.app now-playing look), transport row in
  the family icon style, `degrade` swapping the live blur for the solid
  card as everywhere else.
- Should follow the interaction model decided for the family (E180): the
  play/pause control is the main action, the rest of the card opens the
  details popup carrying the secondary controls (volume, shuffle,
  repeat, seek) — which also solves the small-card crowding problem the
  circle/metro cards have.
- Add to the palette under Glass, the TESTING.md glass list, and the
  family-switch pairing table.

**Relates:** E182 (echo2mqtt discovery + the shared capability gaps), E180
(glass interaction model), E115 (family switch), circle/metro-media
(the contract to mirror).

---

**Shipped (08/2026)** as `@feezal/feezal-element-glass-media`, a thin
VIEW over the E182 controller — the frosted card is essentially chrome
plus a render:

- Album art fills the card with a gradient scrim under the text block
  (the now-playing look), placeholder album glyph when there is no art,
  `degrade` swapping the live blur for the solid card, and media-specific
  size presets (2x2 square, 4x2 wide, 4x1 bar — squarer than the
  standard glass tiles because a media card needs two text lines plus
  the transport row).
- Identical contract and `discovery` component to its siblings, so the
  E115 family switch pairs circle ↔ metro ↔ glass by tag suffix with no
  table to maintain, and the Echo recognizer stamps all three the same.
- Secondary controls (shuffle/repeat, seek) default to OFF here — the
  card stays calm; turning them on is one toggle. When E180 lands, they
  move into the details popup and the card keeps art, metadata and
  play/pause.

Tests: the glass half of `test-browser/feezal-media.test.js` (contract
parity with circle-media, echo wiring, the dedupe rule, transport +
mute, placeholder/degrade, size presets).
