# B91 — Glass cards: badge/details-button placement is inconsistent across the family ✅ fixed
*Archived roadmap item — Bugs. Open items: [../ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) · Index: [README.md](README.md)*

**Reported (07/2026, with screenshot).** Across the glass cards the low-battery, availability and warning badges — and the details (flip) button — landed in **different corners on different cards**, and sometimes collided. In the reporter's row: a contact showed a warning top-right + battery bottom-right; a light showed the details button top-right + a warning bottom-right; a climate showed the details button top-right + battery bottom-left.

**Confirmed cause — three positioning systems, no shared corner:** battery (`feezalBatteryStyles`) defaulted bottom-right (glass-climate overrode to bottom-left); fault + sabotage (`feezalFaultStyles`) defaulted bottom-left; availability (`.unavail`) was **redeclared in every glass element** (top-right in contact/value/motion/sensor, bottom-right in light/climate); the details button (`.flip-btn`) was top-right. Four indicators over four corners, `.unavail` drifting per element, the climate battery override colliding with the bottom-left fault badge, and the top-right `.unavail` colliding with the details button.

**✅ Fixed (07/2026) — one shared upper-right tray.** Added `glassBadgeTray()` + the `.glass-badge-tray` styles to `@feezal/feezal-glass` (`glassCardStyles`, so all cards inherit it). Every glass card now renders its indicators through the tray, anchored to the upper-right corner, **always in the order battery · availability · warnings (fault, then sabotage) · details** — absent indicators render nothing and leave no gap; the order never changes with which are shown. The badges keep their own class-based icons (`.feezal-batt-badge` / `.feezal-fault-badge` / `.feezal-sabotage-badge`) and a shared `.glass-unavail` availability glyph; the tray neutralises their per-family absolute positioning so they are static flex children. The `.flip-btn` moved into the tray as the last (corner) child — its absolute `top/right` positioning was removed from `glassPopupStyles`.

Refactored all 14 glass cards that carry any indicator (value, switch, gauge, loadpoint, contact, motion, sensor, light, cover, wled, lock, meter, fan, climate): removed every per-element `.unavail` block, the glass-climate battery/fault overrides and its B66 bottom-padding hack, and the fan's separate `availabilityBadge`/`feezalAvailabilityStyles` (it now uses the tray's `⚠`, matching the others). The cover's direction badge (top-left) and the meter's inline error text are separate concerns and were left as-is. Other families (circle/metro/eink/panel/tui) are out of scope.

**Tests:** `test-browser/feezal-glass-badge-tray.test.js` asserts every active indicator renders into `.glass-badge-tray` (not a per-card corner) and in the fixed canonical order, plus the details button last on a popup card; the availability/battery/sabotage parity tests still pass. 119 glass browser tests green; full build clean. Patch-bumped `feezal-glass` + the 14 cards.

**Relates:** `@feezal/feezal-glass` (`glassCardStyles`/`glassPopupStyles` + the new `glassBadgeTray`), `feezalBatteryStyles` (`feezal-element.js`) + `feezalFaultStyles` (`feezal-hm-fault.js`) — the shared badge styles whose per-card position vars this removed, **E124**/**E132** (battery), **E135** (fault/sabotage), **B66**/**B67** (earlier glass badge-overlap/parity fixes — same surface), the 14 `feezal-element-glass-*` cards.
