# B116 — Gap-snap helper lines: show only the line being snapped to, not all four ✅ fixed
*Archived roadmap item — Bugs. Open items: [../ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) · Index: [README.md](README.md)*

**Reported (08/2026).** Follow-up on [B113](B113.md) ✅: when a gap snap activates, four helper lines appear. Wanted exactly one — the line at the border of the dragged element where the snap will land. The gap arrows already tell the between-which-edges story; the extra lines only add noise.

## Which of the four survives

B113 drew a line at every end of both spans, which is the complete story and three lines too many. The one that earns its place is the **dragged element's own edge** — where the snap will actually put it — and which edge that is depends on the side the candidate proposes:

- placing **after** the anchor, the dragged box's **leading** edge closes the proposed gap (`proposed.to`);
- placing **before** it — or **centring** between two — its **trailing** edge does (`proposed.from`).

Either way it is the end of the proposed span belonging to the *moving* element rather than to a stationary neighbour, so the proposed arrow still terminates ON the line at a right angle: B113's reading survives at a quarter of the ink.

One line **per axis**, so a position where both rhythms apply ([B112](B112.md) ✅) shows one vertical and one horizontal — and the [U99](U99.md) ✅ position readout rides along on whichever lines remain, now naming the position the element is about to take.

The marker pools shrank with it: `GAP_LINE_COUNT` went from four per axis to one, derived in [feezal-canvas-gaps.js](../../www/src/feezal-canvas-gaps.js) next to the candidate shape it follows from.

**Verified** in the real editor ([editor-snap-guides.test.js](../../www/test-e2e/editor-snap-guides.test.js)): a drag where both rhythms apply shows exactly one vertical and one horizontal line, each still spanning the canvas, each reading the proposed position; a drag in range of one axis shows one line, not four. The before/after edge choice is pinned in [feezal-sidebar-inspector-logic.test.js](../../www/test/feezal-sidebar-inspector-logic.test.js), and taking the wrong end fails the e2e readout.
